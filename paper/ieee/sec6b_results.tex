\subsection{Confidence-Gated Offloading}\label{sec:res-gate}
Fig.~\ref{fig:gate} shows the accuracy--offload trade-off obtained by sweeping $\alpha\in[0.3,1]$ for three values of $\beta$, and Table~\ref{tab:gate} details the a-priori operating point. Station-only inspection never offloads but reaches only F1 0.659 with 28.2\,\% false alarms; edge-only inspection reaches F1 0.753 at 3.4\,\% but offloads every frame. The cascade with $(\alpha,\beta)=(0.6,1.5)$ attains F1 0.748---99.3\,\% of the edge-only value---while offloading 50.9\,\% of the frames, and cuts the false-alarm rate to 7.9\,\% (without grid: F1 0.930 vs.\ 0.935 edge-only and 0.801 station-only). Along the Pareto front, $(0.7,1.5)$ keeps F1 at 0.739 with 39.8\,\% offload and $(0.6,2.0)$ matches edge-only with 61.8\,\%; the lower bound $\alpha$ is the dominant knob.

The gate adapts to the fabric without any tuning (Table~\ref{tab:gate}): it offloads 23\,\% of leather and 30\,\% of wood frames, where the station model is strong, but 81\,\% of carpet frames, where it is weak, and it corrects the station's 95.8\,\% false-alarm rate on leather to 19.8\,\%.
